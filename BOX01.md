## 1. Purpose

To establish requirements for safe box placement, storage, and stacking
to prevent falling-object hazards, workplace obstructions, and
interference with emergency access.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to warehouse storage areas, construction sites,
material-handling zones, packaging areas, and other designated locations
where boxes are stored or handled.

## 3. Policy Rules

### BOX01-01 --- Designated Box Storage

Boxes must be stored within designated storage areas or approved
temporary handling locations. Boxes must not be placed in unauthorized
operational areas.

**Severity:** Medium

### BOX01-02 --- Pedestrian Walkway Protection

Boxes must not partially or completely obstruct designated pedestrian
walkways.

**Severity:** High

### BOX01-03 --- Emergency Exit Access Protection

Boxes must not obstruct emergency exits, evacuation routes, or
designated emergency access paths.

**Severity:** Critical

### BOX01-04 --- Fire Safety Equipment Access

Boxes must not obstruct access to fire extinguishers, fire hose
cabinets, or other designated fire safety equipment.

**Severity:** Critical

### BOX01-05 --- Stable Box Stacking

Boxes must be stacked in a stable arrangement. Apparently unstable,
leaning, or visibly displaced stacks must be inspected and corrected.

**Severity:** High

### BOX01-06 --- Operational Pathway Clearance

Boxes must remain within designated storage boundaries and must not
protrude into operational pathways or vehicle movement areas.

**Severity:** Medium

## 5. Compliance & Enforcement

1.  Every confirmed violation must be associated with its relevant
    clause ID.
2.  Boxes must remain within approved storage boundaries.
3.  Emergency access and fire equipment obstructions require immediate
    corrective action.
4.  Apparently unstable stacks must be physically inspected before being
    declared safe.
5.  Corrective actions must be recorded and verified before a violation
    is closed.
6.  Where designated storage boundaries or operational pathways cannot
    be established from available evidence, verification must be
    requested.
