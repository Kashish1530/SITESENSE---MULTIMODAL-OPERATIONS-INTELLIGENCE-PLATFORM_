## 1. Purpose

To establish requirements for maintaining the visibility and
accessibility of emergency exit signage and associated evacuation access
areas to support safe and timely evacuation.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to designated emergency exit signs, evacuation
routes, exit access paths, and associated emergency access areas in
warehouses, construction sites, and other workplace premises.

## 3. Policy Rules

### EXIT01-01 --- Exit Sign Visibility

Emergency exit signs must remain visible from their designated viewing
directions so that employees and visitors can identify emergency exit
locations.

**Severity:** High

### EXIT01-02 --- Exit Sign Obstruction Prevention

Pallets, boxes, products, equipment, and other objects must not
partially or completely obstruct emergency exit signage.

**Severity:** Critical

### EXIT01-03 --- Emergency Exit Access Clearance

Emergency exit access paths must remain clear of stored materials,
equipment, and other physical obstructions.

**Severity:** Critical

### EXIT01-04 --- Exit Sign Placement Protection

Emergency exit signs must remain in their designated locations and must
not be removed, covered, or repositioned without appropriate
authorization.

**Severity:** High

### EXIT01-05 --- Evacuation Route Protection

Materials and equipment must not obstruct designated evacuation routes
or interfere with access to emergency exits.

**Severity:** Critical

## 5. Compliance & Enforcement

1.  Every confirmed violation must be associated with its relevant
    clause ID.
2.  Emergency exit signs must remain visible from designated viewing
    directions.
3.  Obstructions affecting exit signage or emergency exit access require
    immediate corrective action.
4.  Missing or displaced exit signs must be verified against the
    approved site layout and signage inventory.
5.  Evacuation routes must be checked for physical obstructions before
    being declared clear.
6.  Corrective actions must be recorded and verified before a violation
    is closed.
7.  Where the designated exit location or evacuation route cannot be
    established from available evidence, physical verification must be
    requested.
