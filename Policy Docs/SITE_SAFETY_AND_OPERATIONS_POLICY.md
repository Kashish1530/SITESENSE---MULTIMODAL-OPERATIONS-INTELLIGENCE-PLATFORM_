# SITESENSE PHYSICAL SITE & FIELD OPERATIONS SAFETY POLICY
**Document Code:** POL-SITE-2026-V1  
**Category:** Physical Sites, Construction Zones, Logistics Depots & Service Centers  
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01  
**Target Facilities:** All outdoor operational yards, construction sites, vehicle access points, and service depot grounds.

---

## SECTION 1: SITE ACCESS, VEHICLE TRAFFIC & HEAVY TRANSPORT

### Clause S1.1 — Heavy Transport & Vehicle Access Protocol
All inbound and outbound transport vehicles, commercial delivery trucks, and logistics carriers entering depot aprons, loading gates, or construction sites must be logged at security checkpoints.
- **Governing Class:** `vehicle/truck`
- **Mandatory Requirements:**
  1. Yard speed limit strictly capped at **15 km/h**.
  2. Heavy transport vehicles must deploy wheel chocks immediately upon parking at loading ramps.
  3. Drivers must maintain a minimum **3.0-meter safety separation** from unassigned ground personnel.
- **Violation Severity:** High.

### Clause S1.2 — Forklift & Mobile Equipment Movement in Field Yards
Forklifts, rough-terrain telehandlers, and yard tractors operating across depot yards must travel within demarcated transit lanes.
- **Governing Classes:** `forklift`, `person`
- **Mandatory Requirements:**
  1. Drivers must sound horns when approaching blind corners, door entries, and pedestrian paths.
  2. A minimum **2.5-meter radial separation distance** must be maintained between moving forklifts and pedestrian workers.
- **Violation Severity:** Critical.

---

## SECTION 2: INDUSTRIAL MACHINERY & OPERATING PERIMETERS

### Clause S2.1 — Machinery Safety Guarding & Exclusion Perimeters
All heavy fixed and mobile machinery (generators, conveyors, earthmoving equipment, compactors, shredders) must maintain physical perimeter barriers or clearly painted red safety boundary lines.
- **Governing Classes:** `machinery`, `person`, `obstacle`
- **Mandatory Requirements:**
  1. Personnel must not cross active machinery safety perimeters without an authorized Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) work permit.
  2. Emergency shutoff (E-Stop) buttons must remain unobstructed by loose materials or obstacles within a 1.0-meter radius.
- **Violation Severity:** Critical.

### Clause S2.2 — Ground Hazards & Work Obstacles Demarcation
Any temporary physical barrier, structural post, cable tray, or trench in a site transit path constitutes an obstacle and must be cordoned off.
- **Governing Classes:** `obstacle`, `safety-cone`
- **Mandatory Requirements:**
  1. Obstacles protruding into traffic lanes must be marked by at least two reflective safety cones or high-visibility hazard barricades.
  2. No equipment may be abandoned unassisted in marked site transit routes.
- **Violation Severity:** High.

---

## SECTION 3: PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT (PPE) FOR FIELD SITES

### Clause S3.1 — Site Head Protection (Safety Helmet Mandate)
Every person (employees, contractors, site supervisors, and visitors) present within an active site, loading yard, or construction perimeter must wear a certified, undamaged safety helmet (ANSI Z89.1 / EN 397).
- **Governing Classes:** `person`, `safety-helmet`
- **Mandatory Requirements:**
  1. Helmets must be worn continuously from gate entry to departure.
  2. Chin straps must be engaged when working near open excavation pits or elevated platforms.
- **Violation Severity:** Critical.

### Clause S3.2 — High-Visibility Clothing in Active Yards
All personnel in areas subject to vehicle, truck, or equipment movement must wear a high-visibility safety vest conforming to EN ISO 20471 Class 2 (fluorescent orange or neon yellow with retro-reflective bands).
- **Governing Classes:** `person`, `safety-vest`
- **Mandatory Requirements:**
  1. Vests must be worn over outer garments with front closures fully fastened.
  2. High-vis apparel is required 24/7 across all outdoor yards.
- **Violation Severity:** High.

---

## SECTION 4: SITE SIGNAGE, WARNINGS & EVACUATION ROUTES

### Clause S4.1 — Mandatory Safety Signage Display
All zone boundaries, hazardous storage points, and vehicle crossings must feature standardized safety signage (e.g., "Caution: Heavy Machinery", "Hard Hat Required", "Speed Limit 15 km/h").
- **Governing Class:** `safety-signage`
- **Mandatory Requirements:**
  1. Signs must be mounted at eye level (1.5 m to 1.8 m height).
  2. Signs must remain clean, upright, and legible from a minimum distance of 5.0 meters.
- **Violation Severity:** Moderate.

### Clause S4.2 — Emergency Exit & Gate Signage
All external site egress gates and designated emergency evacuation points must display green/white illuminated or reflective exit signage (ISO 7010).
- **Governing Classes:** `exit-sign`, `obstacle`
- **Mandatory Requirements:**
  1. A 2.0-meter clear zone must be maintained in front of all emergency egress gates. No obstacles, debris, or vehicles may block exit routes.
- **Violation Severity:** Critical.

---

## SECTION 5: OUTDOOR FIRE SUPPRESSION & CHEMICAL HAZARDS

### Clause S5.1 — Site Fire Extinguisher Points
Outdoor fire extinguisher stands must be positioned at every vehicle fueling bay, chemical depot, and main building entrance.
- **Governing Classes:** `safet--fire-extinguisher`, `safety-fire-extinguisher`, `obstacle`
- **Mandatory Requirements:**
  1. Radial clearance of **1.0 meter** around fire extinguishers must be kept clear of all obstacles.
  2. Monthly inspections and valid annual certification tags are mandatory.
- **Violation Severity:** Critical.

### Clause S5.2 — Site Fire Hose Stations
External fire hydrant pillars and hose reel stations must remain unobstructed.
- **Governing Classes:** `fire-hose`, `obstacle`
- **Mandatory Requirements:**
  1. A **1.5-meter clear approach perimeter** must be preserved.
- **Violation Severity:** Critical.

### Clause S5.3 — Uncontrolled Fire & Combustion Emergency
Any detection of uncontrolled open flames, chemical combustion, or thick smoke in an outdoor yard or building represents a Level 1 Life-Safety Emergency.
- **Governing Class:** `fire`
- **Action Protocol:** Immediate sounding of yard sirens, automated gate opening for emergency vehicles, and muster station headcounts.
- **Violation Severity:** Immediate Emergency (Critical).

### Clause S5.4 — Field Spill Management & Caution Cones
Fuel, hydraulic fluid, oil, or chemical leaks exceeding 0.5 liters must be contained within 15 minutes of occurrence.
- **Governing Classes:** `spill`, `safety-cone`
- **Mandatory Requirements:**
  1. The spill perimeter must be immediately cordoned off with a minimum of **two safety cones**.
  2. Neutralization using outdoor absorbent spill kits must be initiated immediately.
- **Violation Severity:** Critical.
