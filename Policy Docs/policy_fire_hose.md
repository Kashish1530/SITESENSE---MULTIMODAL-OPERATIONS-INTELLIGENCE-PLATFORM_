## 1. Purpose

To establish rules for ensuring that all fixed fire hose reels and hose cabinets remain complete, properly stowed, unobstructed, and ready for immediate emergency deployment.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all wall-mounted and recessed fire hose reels, hose cabinets, standpipe connections, and adjacent walkways across warehouses, plant floors, and commercial corridors.

## 3. Policy Rules

### HOSE01-01 — Complete Assembly & Station Presence

Every designated fire hose station must contain a complete, intact fire hose assembly with nozzle attached and isolation valve accessible at all times.

**Severity:** Critical

### HOSE01-02 — Access Clearance & Cabinet Door Swing

A minimum 1.5-meter (5-foot) unobstructed access clearance and full 180-degree cabinet door swing radius must be maintained free of stored goods, pallets, or parked equipment.

**Severity:** High

### HOSE01-03 — Proper Coiling & Walkway Hazard Prevention

When not in emergency use, the fire hose must remain neatly coiled on its reel or racked inside its cabinet. Uncoiled or trailing hoses across walkways constitute both readiness and trip hazards.

**Severity:** Medium

### HOSE01-04 — Cabinet Door & Signage Visibility

Fire hose cabinet doors must remain closed (or fully unobstructed if open-reel) with clear identification signage visible. Cabinets must never be locked with unauthorized padlocks or blocked by covers.

**Severity:** High

### HOSE01-05 — Post-Deployment Inspection & Re-Stowage

Following any emergency deployment, drill, or maintenance test, the fire hose must be drained, pressure-checked, properly re-coiled, and certified ready before normal zone operations resume.

**Severity:** High

## 4. Violation Triggers & Required Actions

| Clause ID | Violation Condition | Severity | Required Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| HOSE01-01 | Fire hose missing or detached from designated reel/cabinet station | Critical | Immediate fire-safety alert and dispatch fire marshal/facilities |
| HOSE01-02 | Obstruction within 1.5-meter clearance or cabinet door swing zone | High | Raise safety/housekeeping ticket for immediate clearance removal |
| HOSE01-03 | Fire hose uncoiled or trailing on floor during non-emergency period | Medium | Notify facilities/safety team to inspect and properly re-coil hose |
| HOSE01-04 | Fire hose cabinet blocked, obscured, or improperly secured | High | Issue urgent compliance ticket to restore unobstructed access |
| HOSE01-05 | Hose station left unready or uninspected after drill or maintenance | High | Suspend clearance and request physical fire-safety verification |

## 5. Compliance & Enforcement

1. Every confirmed violation must be associated with its relevant clause ID.
2. Corrective actions must be recorded and tracked until completion.
3. A station must not be marked compliant solely because an obstruction or trailing hose is no longer visible in a later observation.
4. Where visual evidence is insufficient to confirm a violation or its resolution, physical verification must be requested.
