## 1. Purpose

To establish rules for monitoring personnel presence, enforcing access boundaries, verifying personal protective equipment (PPE) pairing, and preventing unauthorized or unsafe occupancy in active operational zones.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, visitors, and vision-monitored operational zones including active machinery bays, restricted exclusion areas, loading docks, and production floors.

## 3. Policy Rules

### PERSON01-01 — Restricted Exclusion Zone Prohibition

No person shall enter or remain inside designated red-lined exclusion zones (such as active crane swing radii, robotic cells, or high-voltage enclosures) while equipment is energized or operational.

**Severity:** Critical

### PERSON01-02 — Authorized Shift & Access Clearance

All personnel detected inside controlled operational zones must hold valid shift authorization and access clearance. Unscheduled or after-hours personnel presence is prohibited.

**Severity:** High

### PERSON01-03 — Mandatory PPE Pairing Compliance

Every person detected inside a designated mandatory PPE zone must simultaneously wear required head protection (`safety_helmet`) and high-visibility apparel (`safety_vest`).

**Severity:** High

### PERSON01-04 — High-Risk Lone Worker Restriction

No person shall work alone in designated high-risk or isolated operational areas without active buddy pairing or authorized lone-worker supervision clearance.

**Severity:** Medium

### PERSON01-05 — Safe Occupancy & Corridor Flow

Personnel must remain within marked pedestrian walkways and must not loiter or obstruct active forklift routes, emergency exits, or heavy equipment operating lanes.

**Severity:** Medium

## 4. Violation Triggers & Required Actions

| Clause ID | Violation Condition | Severity | Required Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| PERSON01-01 | Person detected inside active restricted exclusion zone | Critical | Immediate safety alert, halt zone operation, and dispatch safety officer |
| PERSON01-02 | Unauthorized or after-hours person detected in controlled zone | High | Notify site security and responsible supervisor for immediate verification |
| PERSON01-03 | Person detected in active PPE zone without required helmet or vest | High | Raise compliance ticket and notify zone supervisor for PPE enforcement |
| PERSON01-04 | Single person detected working alone in designated high-risk zone | Medium | Alert zone supervisor to verify buddy check-in or lone-worker clearance |
| PERSON01-05 | Person obstructing active vehicle lane or outside pedestrian walkway | Medium | Issue walkway compliance alert to zone supervisor |

## 5. Compliance & Enforcement

1. Every confirmed violation must be associated with its relevant clause ID.
2. Corrective actions must be recorded and tracked until completion.
3. An area must not be marked compliant solely because an unauthorized person is no longer visible in a later observation.
4. Where visual evidence is insufficient to confirm authorization or resolution, physical verification must be requested.
