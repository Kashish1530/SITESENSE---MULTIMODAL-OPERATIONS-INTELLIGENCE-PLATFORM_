## 1. Purpose

To establish rules for continuous head protection compliance to prevent head injuries caused by falling objects, overhead hazards, low-clearance structures, and electrical contact across workplace zones.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and visitors entering construction zones, warehouse aisles, loading docks, overhead crane areas, and active plant floors.

## 3. Policy Rules

### HELMET01-01 — Continuous Head Protection in Mandatory Zones

Every person entering or working inside a designated mandatory hard-hat zone must wear an approved safety helmet securely on the head at all times.

**Severity:** High

### HELMET01-02 — Proper Helmet Placement & Fastening

Safety helmets must be worn properly positioned on the head with chin straps fastened where required. Carrying a helmet in hand or wearing it improperly does not satisfy compliance.

**Severity:** Medium

### HELMET01-03 — Zero-Grace Removal Prohibition

Personnel must not remove their safety helmet while inside an active operational or overhead-hazard zone, even briefly, unless inside an enclosed exempt office or break room.

**Severity:** High

### HELMET01-04 — Equipment Integrity & Certification Standard

All safety helmets used on site must conform to IS 2925 / ANSI Z89.1 standards and remain free of cracks, dents, or structural damage. Damaged helmets must be replaced immediately.

**Severity:** Medium

### HELMET01-05 — Elevated & Overhead Work Enforcement

Personnel operating beneath active overhead lifting, scaffolding, or elevated work platforms must maintain uninterrupted safety helmet compliance until clear of the hazard zone.

**Severity:** Critical

## 4. Violation Triggers & Required Actions

| Clause ID | Violation Condition | Severity | Required Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| HELMET01-01 | Person detected in mandatory hard-hat zone without a safety helmet | High | Raise PPE violation ticket and notify zone supervisor immediately |
| HELMET01-02 | Safety helmet detected carried in hand or worn improperly in active zone | Medium | Log compliance warning and notify supervisor for corrective coaching |
| HELMET01-03 | Safety helmet removed while person remains inside active work zone | High | Issue immediate PPE alert and record violation in audit trail |
| HELMET01-04 | Visually damaged or non-standard helmet identified in operational area | Medium | Require immediate helmet replacement before work resumes |
| HELMET01-05 | Person beneath active overhead hazard without safety helmet | Critical | Immediate safety alert and urgent supervisor intervention |

## 5. Compliance & Enforcement

1. Every confirmed violation must be associated with its relevant clause ID.
2. Corrective actions must be recorded and tracked until completion.
3. A zone must not be marked compliant solely because a non-compliant person exits the camera view in a later observation.
4. Where visual evidence is insufficient to confirm a violation or its resolution, physical verification must be requested.
