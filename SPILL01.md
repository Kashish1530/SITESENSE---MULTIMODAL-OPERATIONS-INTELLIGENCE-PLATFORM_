## 1. Purpose

To establish requirements for identifying, reporting, isolating, and
managing spills in workplace areas to prevent slip hazards, unsafe
movement, and workplace accidents.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to warehouse floors, construction work areas,
material-handling zones, pedestrian walkways, and other operational
areas where spills may create safety risks.

## 3. Policy Rules

### SPILL01-01 --- Prompt Spill Reporting

All identified spills in operational areas must be reported promptly to
the responsible supervisor or safety personnel.

**Severity:** Medium

### SPILL01-02 --- Pedestrian Walkway Protection

Spills must not remain uncontrolled in pedestrian walkways. Affected
areas must be isolated until the spill has been appropriately cleaned
and declared safe.

**Severity:** High

### SPILL01-03 --- Vehicle Operating Area Protection

Spills in forklift routes or vehicle operating areas must be isolated.
Vehicle movement through the affected area must remain restricted until
the area is confirmed safe.

**Severity:** High

### SPILL01-04 --- Emergency Access Protection

Spills must not compromise access to emergency exits, evacuation routes,
fire extinguishers, or other emergency safety equipment.

**Severity:** Critical

### SPILL01-05 --- Safe Area Restoration

Normal operations must not resume in an affected area until the spill
has been appropriately cleaned, inspected, and declared safe by
authorized personnel.

**Severity:** High

## 5. Compliance & Enforcement

1.  Every confirmed violation must be associated with its relevant
    clause ID.
2.  Corrective actions must be recorded and tracked until completion.
3.  An area must not be marked compliant solely because a spill is no
    longer visible in a later observation.
4.  Where visual evidence is insufficient to confirm a violation or its
    resolution, physical verification must be requested.
