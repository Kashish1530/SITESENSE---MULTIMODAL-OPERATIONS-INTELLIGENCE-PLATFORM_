## 1. Purpose

To establish requirements for safe pallet placement, storage, and
handling to prevent workplace obstructions, unstable storage conditions,
and interference with emergency access.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to employees, contractors, and visitors in warehouse
storage areas, construction sites, material-handling zones, and other
designated pallet storage locations.

## 3. Policy Rules

### PALLET01-01 --- Designated Pallet Storage

Pallets must be stored in designated storage areas or approved temporary
handling locations. Pallets must not be placed in unauthorized
operational areas.

**Severity:** Medium

### PALLET01-02 --- Pedestrian Walkway Protection

Pallets must not partially or completely obstruct designated pedestrian
walkways.

**Severity:** High

### PALLET01-03 --- Emergency Exit Access Protection

Pallets must not obstruct emergency exits, evacuation routes, or
designated emergency access paths.

**Severity:** Critical

### PALLET01-04 --- Fire Safety Equipment Access

Pallets must not obstruct access to fire extinguishers, fire hose
cabinets, or other designated fire safety equipment.

**Severity:** Critical

### PALLET01-05 --- Stable Pallet Stacking

Pallets must be stacked and positioned in a stable manner. Apparently
unstable or leaning stacks must be inspected and corrected before the
area is returned to normal use.

**Severity:** High

## 5. Compliance & Enforcement

1.  Every confirmed violation must be associated with its relevant
    clause ID.
2.  Pallets must be relocated when found outside approved storage
    locations.
3.  Emergency access and fire equipment obstructions require immediate
    corrective action.
4.  Apparently unstable stacking must be physically inspected before
    being declared safe.
5.  Corrective actions must be recorded and verified before a violation
    is closed.
6.  Where designated storage boundaries or access paths cannot be
    established from available evidence, verification must be requested.
