## 1. Purpose

To establish requirements for maintaining the visibility, accessibility,
and proper placement of workplace safety signage so that warnings,
instructions, and mandatory precautions remain identifiable.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to designated safety signage installed in
warehouses, construction sites, storage areas, operational zones, and
restricted workplace areas.

## 3. Policy Rules

### SIGN01-01 --- Signage Visibility

Safety signage must remain visible from its designated viewing
directions so that employees and visitors can identify the displayed
safety information.

**Severity:** Medium

### SIGN01-02 --- Obstruction Prevention

Pallets, boxes, products, equipment, and other stored materials must not
partially or completely obstruct safety signage.

**Severity:** High

### SIGN01-03 --- Warning Sign Visibility

Warning and hazard signage installed in active operational areas must
remain visible and must not be hidden behind stored materials or
equipment.

**Severity:** High

### SIGN01-04 --- Designated Signage Placement

Safety signage must remain in its assigned location. Signs must not be
moved, removed, or repositioned without appropriate authorization.

**Severity:** Medium

### SIGN01-05 --- Safety Message Accessibility

The essential message, symbols, and instructions displayed on safety
signage must remain sufficiently unobstructed to allow the intended
information to be understood.

**Severity:** Medium

## 5. Compliance & Enforcement

1.  Every confirmed violation must be associated with its relevant
    clause ID.
2.  Obstructions must be removed promptly to restore signage visibility.
3.  Warning signs in active operational areas require priority
    corrective action.
4.  Signs must be restored to approved locations following authorized
    maintenance or replacement.
5.  Corrective actions must be recorded and verified.
6.  Missing signage must be verified against the approved site signage
    inventory before being classified as a violation.
7.  Where the designated viewing direction or assigned sign location is
    unknown, verification must be requested.
