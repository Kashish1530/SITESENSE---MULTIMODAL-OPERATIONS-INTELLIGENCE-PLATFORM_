# SITESENSE WAREHOUSE STORAGE & DISTRIBUTION CENTER POLICY
**Document Code:** POL-WH-2026-V1  
**Category:** Warehouses, Fulfillment Centers, Logistics Hubs & Retail Superstores  
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01  
**Target Facilities:** All indoor storage floors, picking aisles, pallet racking bays, packing stations, and loading docks.

---

## SECTION 1: FORKLIFT TRAFFIC, AISLE PROTOCOLS & PEDESTRIANS

### Clause W1.1 — Forklift Safe Operating Distance & Pedestrian Separation
Forklifts, reach trucks, and electric pallet jacks must operate exclusively within designated painted vehicular transit lanes on warehouse floors.
- **Governing Classes:** `forklift`, `person`
- **Mandatory Requirements:**
  1. A minimum **2.5-meter (8-foot) separation distance** must be preserved between any operating forklift and pedestrian personnel.
  2. Forklift operators must come to a complete stop if a pedestrian approaches within 2.5 meters.
  3. Reversing forklifts must feature operational backup beepers and flashing blue pedestrian warning spotlights.
- **Violation Severity:** Critical.

### Clause W1.2 — High-Visibility Apparel in Warehouse Transit Aisles
Every warehouse operative, inventory auditor, or picker working in or crossing main transit aisles with forklift traffic must wear a certified high-visibility safety vest (EN ISO 20471).
- **Governing Classes:** `person`, `safety-vest`
- **Violation Severity:** High.

---

## SECTION 2: PALLET HANDLING, RACKING INTEGRITY & AISLE CLEARANCE

### Clause W2.1 — Primary Walkway Minimum Clearance
All marked pedestrian walkways, emergency corridors, and aisle intersections must maintain a continuous minimum unobstructed clearance width of **1.2 meters (4.0 feet)**.
- **Governing Classes:** `pallets`, `obstacle`, `person`
- **Mandatory Requirements:**
  1. Staging wooden pallets, plastic totes, or loose boxes in yellow-marked pedestrian transit lanes is strictly prohibited.
  2. Any pallet or obstacle detected in a pedestrian corridor constitutes an immediate compliance failure.
- **Violation Severity:** High.

### Clause W2.2 — Pallet Stacking Height & Shrink-Wrap Stability
Freestanding pallet unit loads staged on warehouse staging floors must adhere to strict stability and height thresholds.
- **Governing Class:** `pallets`
- **Mandatory Requirements:**
  1. Maximum freestanding pallet stack height is capped at **2.4 meters (8.0 feet)** or three unit tiers, whichever is lower.
  2. Pallets must be uniformly shrink-wrapped with no leaning exceeding 5 degrees from the vertical.
  3. Empty pallets must be stacked flat (max 15 pallets high) and never stored vertically on their sides.
- **Violation Severity:** High.

### Clause W2.3 — Emergency Fire Exit Zero-Tolerance Clearance
Warehouse emergency exit doors, panic push-bars, and immediate escape paths leading outdoors must remain **100% unobstructed at all times**.
- **Governing Classes:** `exit-sign`, `obstacle`, `pallets`
- **Mandatory Requirements:**
  1. Zero tolerance: Storing, staging, or momentarily parking any pallet, cardboard carton, cart, or obstacle within **2.0 meters** of an emergency exit door is strictly prohibited.
  2. Exit doors must never be padlocked or wedged open.
- **Violation Severity:** Critical (immediate priority remediation ticket).

## SECTION 3: INVENTORY MONITORING & SHELF STOCKING (RETAIL / DC)

### Clause W3.1 — Shelf Stocking Compliance & Empty Shelf Rectification
In retail distribution centers, active pick faces, and retail store customer aisles, empty shelf space must be continuously monitored and replenished.
- **Governing Class:** `empty-shelf`
- **Mandatory Requirements:**
  1. Empty shelf area must not exceed **15% of the total shelf module face** during operating hours.
  2. When an empty shelf section is detected, a stock replenishment task must be executed within **2 hours**.
  3. Gaps exceeding 30% of a bay indicate severe out-of-stock conditions requiring immediate supervisor escalation.
- **Violation Severity:** Moderate.

---

## SECTION 4: WAREHOUSE FIRE SUPPRESSION & LIFE-SAFETY SYSTEMS

### Clause W4.1 — Fire Extinguisher Accessibility & Radial Clearance
Portable fire extinguishers (ABC dry powder, CO2, foam) must be mounted on warehouse columns or designated wall backboards with red identification markers.
- **Governing Classes:** `safet--fire-extinguisher`, `safety-fire-extinguisher`, `obstacle`, `pallets`
- **Mandatory Requirements:**
  1. A minimum radial clearance of **1.0 meter (3.3 feet)** around each extinguisher must be kept completely free of pallet stacks, cartons, and obstacles.
  2. Every extinguisher must carry a valid inspection tag recording monthly checks within the past 365 days.
- **Violation Severity:** Critical.

### Clause W4.2 — Fire Hose Reel Station Unobstructed Access
Warehouse high-pressure fire hose reel stations must remain fully accessible.
- **Governing Classes:** `fire-hose`, `obstacle`, `pallets`
- **Mandatory Requirements:**
  1. A clear access zone of **1.5 meters wide by 1.5 meters deep** directly in front of each hose reel cabinet is mandatory.
- **Violation Severity:** Critical.

### Clause W4.3 — Indoor Active Fire & Smoke Suppression Protocol
Any detection of active flame, smoldering combustible goods, or smoke inside the facility constitutes an immediate catastrophic hazard.
- **Governing Class:** `fire`
- **Mandatory Requirements:**
  1. Automatic fire system triggering, central alarm notification, air handling unit shutdown, and immediate facility-wide evacuation.
- **Violation Severity:** Immediate Emergency (Critical).

## SECTION 5: HOUSEKEEPING, SPILLS & HAZARD DEMARCATION

### Clause W5.1 — Liquid Spill Containment Protocol
Any liquid spill (water, cleaning chemical, hydraulic fluid, packaging puncture) exceeding 0.5 liters presents an immediate slip hazard.
- **Governing Classes:** `spill`, `safety-cone`
- **Mandatory Requirements:**
  1. The spill zone must be cordoned off with high-visibility safety cones within **15 minutes** of detection.
  2. Spill kit absorbent booms and pads must be deployed until the surface is completely dry and certified safe for foot traffic.
- **Violation Severity:** Critical (slip, trip, and fall hazard).

### Clause W5.2 — Mandatory Safety Cone Placement for Floor Hazards
Whenever a wet floor, ongoing maintenance repair, or surface hazard exists on a warehouse floor, at least **two safety cones** must be deployed around the perimeter.
- **Governing Classes:** `safety-cone`, `spill`, `obstacle`
- **Violation Severity:** High.

---

## SECTION 6: WAREHOUSE PPE COMPLIANCE

### Clause W6.1 — Warehouse Floor Head Protection
Every employee, temporary worker, or visitor present inside active warehouse storage zones, racking aisles, picking bays, or packing areas must wear an approved safety helmet (ANSI Z89.1 / EN 397).
- **Governing Classes:** `person`, `safety-helmet`
- **Violation Severity:** Critical.

---

## SECTION 7: WAREHOUSE SIGNAGE & DIRECTIONAL INDICATORS

### Clause W7.1 — Illuminated Exit Signage Integrity
ISO 7010 green running-man exit signs must be mounted above all warehouse egress doors and at major aisle transitions. Signs must remain illuminated 24/7 with zero visual blockage from tall pallet racks or hanging plastic curtains.
- **Governing Class:** `exit-sign`
- **Violation Severity:** Critical.

### Clause W7.2 — Facility Safety & Warning Signage Display
Mandatory safety signage (e.g., "Forklift Crossing", "Max Load 2000kg", "Pedestrian Walkway Only") must be visibly mounted at every aisle entrance and zone gateway.
- **Governing Class:** `safety-signage`
- **Violation Severity:** Moderate.

---

## SECTION 8: AUTOMATED COMPLIANCE TICKETING & APPROVAL GATE

### Clause W8.1 — Remediation Ticket Generation
Automated inspection findings rated High or Critical automatically generate a proposed remediation ticket in the system. The ticket remains in `proposed` status until a Warehouse Shift Manager or Safety Officer reviews and explicitly approves it in the UI.
- **Violation Severity:** High.
