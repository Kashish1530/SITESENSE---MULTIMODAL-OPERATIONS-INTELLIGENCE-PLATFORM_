## 1. Purpose

To establish rules for maintaining the presence, unobstructed accessibility, physical mounting, and operational readiness of portable fire extinguishers across all workplace facilities.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all designated fire extinguisher stations across production floors, warehouses, electrical rooms, corridors, offices, and storage zones.

## 3. Policy Rules

### EXT01-01 — Designated Station Presence

Every designated fire safety point must maintain its assigned fire extinguisher mounted on its bracket or floor stand at all times during normal operations.

**Severity:** Critical

### EXT01-02 — Unobstructed Access Clearance Radius

A minimum 1-meter (3-foot) unobstructed clearance radius must be maintained around every fire extinguisher. Pallets, cartons, machinery, or waste bins must not block access.

**Severity:** High

### EXT01-03 — Upright Mounting & Physical Stability

Fire extinguishers must remain securely mounted upright in designated holders or stands. Units found lying on the floor, knocked over, or displaced must be inspected and remounted immediately.

**Severity:** High

### EXT01-04 — Inspection Tag & Pressure Readiness

Every fire extinguisher must display a current monthly inspection tag, intact safety pin and tamper seal, and a pressure gauge needle within the green operational zone.

**Severity:** Medium

### EXT01-05 — Post-Use & Maintenance Replacement

Any fire extinguisher removed for discharge, servicing, or hydrostatic testing must be immediately replaced with a fully charged spare unit of equivalent rating.

**Severity:** Critical

## 4. Violation Triggers & Required Actions

| Clause ID | Violation Condition | Severity | Required Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| EXT01-01 | Expected fire extinguisher missing from designated station ROI | Critical | Immediate fire-safety alert and dispatch facilities/fire marshal |
| EXT01-02 | Obstruction detected within 1-meter clearance zone of fire extinguisher | High | Raise housekeeping/safety ticket for immediate obstruction removal |
| EXT01-03 | Fire extinguisher detected knocked over or displaced on floor | High | Notify facilities team to inspect unit and restore proper mounting |
| EXT01-04 | Missing inspection tag, broken seal, or expired inspection status | Medium | Schedule urgent fire-equipment inspection and compliance update |
| EXT01-05 | Station left empty during maintenance or post-discharge without spare | Critical | Immediate safety alert and deploy replacement extinguisher |

## 5. Compliance & Enforcement

1. Every confirmed violation must be associated with its relevant clause ID.
2. Corrective actions must be recorded and tracked until completion.
3. A station must not be marked compliant solely because an obstruction is temporarily outside the camera frame in a later observation.
4. Where visual evidence is insufficient to confirm equipment readiness or resolution, physical verification must be requested.
