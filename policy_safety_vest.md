## 1. Purpose

To establish rules for high-visibility apparel compliance so all personnel remain clearly visible to forklift operators, crane drivers, and moving vehicles across operational and low-light areas.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and visitors operating in warehouse aisles, loading bays, outdoor yards, roadwork zones, and shared pedestrian-vehicle corridors.

## 3. Policy Rules

### VEST01-01 — Mandatory High-Visibility Vest in Traffic Zones

Every person entering or working within forklift routes, loading bays, or vehicle operating areas must wear an approved fluorescent, retro-reflective safety vest (Class 2 or Class 3 equivalent).

**Severity:** High

### VEST01-02 — Unobstructed Outermost Layer Requirement

The safety vest must be worn as the outermost garment and fastened securely in the front so that fluorescent panels and reflective strips remain fully visible from all angles.

**Severity:** Medium

### VEST01-03 — Garment Condition & Reflectivity Standard

Safety vests must be clean, intact, and free of tears, heavy soiling, or faded reflective strips that reduce visibility. Defective vests must be replaced prior to shift entry.

**Severity:** Low

### VEST01-04 — Loading Dock & Yard Night-Shift Visibility

Personnel working in outdoor yards, dimly lit storage areas, or night shifts must maintain uninterrupted high-visibility vest compliance at all times while outside enclosed buildings.

**Severity:** High

### VEST01-05 — Shared Vehicle-Pedestrian Crossing Compliance

No person shall cross or walk along active material-handling lanes without wearing a properly fastened high-visibility safety vest.

**Severity:** Critical

## 4. Violation Triggers & Required Actions

| Clause ID | Violation Condition | Severity | Required Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| VEST01-01 | Person detected in forklift or vehicle operating zone without safety vest | High | Raise PPE violation ticket and notify zone supervisor immediately |
| VEST01-02 | Safety vest worn unfastened or covered by outer jacket or backpack | Medium | Log visibility warning and instruct worker to adjust outer garment |
| VEST01-03 | Torn, heavily soiled, or faded safety vest observed in operational zone | Low | Notify supervisor to issue replacement high-visibility vest |
| VEST01-04 | Person in outdoor yard or low-light zone without safety vest | High | Issue immediate safety alert and arrange PPE compliance check |
| VEST01-05 | Person crossing active vehicle lane without high-visibility vest | Critical | Immediate safety alert and urgent corrective action |

## 5. Compliance & Enforcement

1. Every confirmed violation must be associated with its relevant clause ID.
2. Corrective actions must be recorded and tracked until completion.
3. An area must not be marked compliant solely because a person without a safety vest is no longer visible in a later observation.
4. Where visual evidence is insufficient to confirm a violation or its resolution, physical verification must be requested.
